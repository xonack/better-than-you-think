Is literacy increasing worldwide?

Yes. In 1820 about 12% of adults could read and write. Today about 87% can.

182012%
Today87%

Most of the rise came after 1950, when basic schooling spread. World literacy was about 42% in 1960 and about 70% in 1983.

Still unsolved: In some countries of sub-Saharan Africa, fewer than 1 in 3 adults can read and write.
