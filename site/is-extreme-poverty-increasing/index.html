Is extreme poverty increasing?

No. The share of people in extreme poverty (less than $3 a day) fell from 36% in 2000 to 10% in 2024.

200036%
202410%

Extreme poverty is measured by the World Bank against a line of $3 a day in 2021 prices. On that line, more than a third of humanity was extremely poor in 2000. By 2024 it was one in ten.

Most people do not know this. In Gapminder surveys across 14 countries, only about 9% of respondents knew that extreme poverty had almost halved in 20 years. Random guessing scores 33%.

Still unsolved: About 847 million people still live on less than $3 a day.
