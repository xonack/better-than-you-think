Put the quiz on your site

"Better Than You Think" is a two-minute quiz on five measures of human progress. Readers guess first, then see the data, the source, and what is still unsolved. It is free, MIT licensed, and sets no cookies.

Embed code

Replace yoursite with a short name, so you can see your own readers in the results below.

<iframe src="https://better-than-you-think.vercel.app/?embed=1&ref=yoursite"
  title="Better Than You Think: a quiz on human progress"
  style="width:100%;height:900px;border:0" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Does it change minds? Live results

Each reader is randomly asked "Is the world getting better or worse?" either before the quiz or after it. The difference between the two groups measures the quiz's effect. Only completed quizzes count.

–completed quizzes
–say "getting better" before the quiz
–say "getting better" after the quiz

Raw data: /api/stats. Small samples are noisy, so read the effect only after a few hundred completions per group.
