Is child mortality going down?

Yes. Deaths before age 5 fell from 93 per 1,000 births in 1990 to 37 per 1,000 in 2023, a drop of about 60%.

199093 per 1,000
202337 per 1,000

In 1990, about 12.5 million children died before their fifth birthday. In 2023, about 4.8 million did, while the number of births stayed large.

Nearly half of the deaths that remain happen in the first 28 days of life, so newborn care is now where most lives can be saved.

Still unsolved: 4.8 million children still died before age 5 in 2023, and since 2015 the rate of decline has slowed by 42% compared with 2000 to 2015.
