Is life expectancy increasing?

Yes. Average life expectancy for the whole world rose from about 46.5 years in 1950 to 73 years in 2023.

195046.5 years
202373 years

In 1900 the global average was about 32 years. It has more than doubled since then. A large part of the gain comes from fewer children dying young.

Still unsolved: The gap between the countries with the longest and shortest lives is still more than 20 years.
