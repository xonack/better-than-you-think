Is solar power getting cheaper?

Yes. Electricity from new large solar plants fell from $0.417 per kWh in 2010 to $0.043 per kWh in 2024, about 90% cheaper.

2010$0.417 / kWh
2024$0.043 / kWh

The total cost to install a large solar plant fell from $5,283 per kW in 2010 to $691 per kW in 2024. In 2024, new solar was on average cheaper than new fossil-fuel power.

Still unsolved: Cheap power is not yet clean power everywhere. Grids, storage and financing still limit how fast it is built.
