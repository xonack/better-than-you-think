Better Than You Think

Is the world getting worse?

On the long-run measures of human welfare, no. Poverty, child deaths, life expectancy, literacy and clean-energy cost have all improved for decades. Progress is not finished, and some of it has slowed.

The news reports events, and slow improvement is not an event. That is one reason most people answer questions about global trends worse than random guessing.
